Opening a new campus? Plan the cafeteria without a wash room

Floor space in a new campus is leased by the square foot. A dish-wash area takes space, plumbing, drainage and a team of people, all in the part of the building nobody sees.

Questions to settle before fit-out

  • How many seats and meals per day at full occupancy?
  • Will the caterer or the company own the wash equipment?
  • Can washing happen offsite, with clean ware delivered daily?

If the answer to the last question is yes, the cafeteria design gets simpler and the caterer carries less capex and labour.
