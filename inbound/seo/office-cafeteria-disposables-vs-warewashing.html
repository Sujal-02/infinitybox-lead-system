Office cafeteria: single-use disposables or reusable ware?

Disposables look cheap per item, but the cost is paid every meal, along with storage, waste handling and the sustainability questions your ESG team will ask.

What to compare

  • Cost per meal, all-in (ware, labour, water, detergent, waste removal)
  • Meals per day and how peaky lunch is
  • Space for a wash area on site
  • Who carries the capex: you, or your caterer

Offsite warewashing moves washing to a dedicated facility, so the cafeteria needs no wash room or capex, and reusable ware replaces single-use items.
