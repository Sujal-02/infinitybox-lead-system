1Clean steel arrivesThalis, katoris and tumblers replace paper plates, plastic cups and boxes.
2Used ware is collectedAfter service, crates of used crockery and containers are picked up.
3Washed properlyAt a central facility, or by a team running dishwashing on your premises.

What is outsourced warewashing?

Instead of buying disposables or washing in your own kitchen, used crockery is collected, washed by a specialist and returned. InfinityBox does this at central facilities, or runs dishwashing inside your premises.
